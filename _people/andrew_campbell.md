### About Me

Andrew Campbell is a Ph.D. student in Electrical and Computer Engineering at Cornell University. He earned a B.S. in Mathematics and a B.S. in Computer Science with a minor in Philosophy from the University of Utah in 2021, where he was a research assistant in the USmart Lab. His research is on differential privacy for networked systems and power grid data, decentralized and federated learning, and graph transfer learning. He is part of the [GridMind](https://gridmind.ece.cornell.edu/) team.
