### About Me

Nadav Harel is a postdoctoral researcher in the SINE Lab, where he works on modeling the power demand of AI data centers and its interaction with the grid. He received his Ph.D. from Ben-Gurion University of the Negev in 2024 with a thesis on post-selection estimation theory, advised by Tirza Routtenberg. His research is in statistical signal processing, estimation and detection theory, and estimation under model misspecification.
