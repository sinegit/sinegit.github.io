### About Me

Chenyue Zhang is a postdoctoral researcher in the SINE Lab. She received her Ph.D. from the Department of Systems Engineering and Engineering Management at the Chinese University of Hong Kong, advised by Hoi-To Wai (a SINE Lab alumnus), and her bachelor's degree in Control Science and Engineering from Zhejiang University. Her research is on graph signal processing, differential privacy, network dynamics and games, graph neural networks, and optimization for machine learning. She received the 2025 ICASSP Best Student Paper Award.
